What is SoundSmart?

Interpretation that plays at exactly the right moment

SoundSmart devices are discreet audio units installed inside signs, panels or display posts. When a visitor approaches - or presses a button - high-quality audio plays instantly, telling them what they're looking at, why it matters, and how it connects to place, history or nature.

When they move on, the sound stops. No continuous loop, no audio spill into adjacent exhibits, and no need for visitors to download anything or find a Wi-Fi network.

Designed for museums, councils, tourism destinations and public spaces - wherever interpretation is key to the visitor experience.

How It Works

Simple, reliable technology

No complexity, no ongoing tech costs. SoundSmart devices are installed inside existing signage or panels and works reliably for years.

01

Visitor approaches or presses a button

The proximity sensor detects someone nearby and triggers playback automatically - or the visitor chooses to press a button. No phone, no app, no login required.

02

The right audio plays instantly

Clear, high-quality audio explains what they're seeing - what it is, why it's significant, and how it connects to place, history or nature.

03

Silence returns naturally

Once the visitor moves on, sound stops. No looping, no disruption to adjacent exhibits or spaces - the environment stays as it should be.
